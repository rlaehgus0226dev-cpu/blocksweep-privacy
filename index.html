Privacy Policy for BlockSweep

Last updated: September 6, 2026

BlockSweep (“the App”) is developed and published by Deokgu House (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the App handles and why. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.

1. Information collected

We ourselves do not operate any server and do not receive your personal data. However, the App displays advertising provided by Google AdMob, and the AdMob software development kit collects the following information from your device:

Data Purpose
Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) Serving ads, measuring ad performance, limiting how often the same ad is shown, and detecting fraud
Approximate location derived from IP address, device model, operating system version, language, screen size Selecting ads appropriate for the device and region
Ad interaction events (an ad was shown, an ad was tapped) Measuring ad performance and calculating payments

Your game progress — high score, current board, sound settings — is stored only in the App’s local storage on your own device. It is never transmitted anywhere, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App.

2. Third-party services

The App uses Google AdMob. Google processes the data described above as an independent controller under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

3. Your choices

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time on your device: Settings → Google → Ads. Deleting the Advertising ID stops personalized advertising; you will still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before using your data for personalized advertising, through Google’s User Messaging Platform. You may change or withdraw that consent at any time from within the App.

4. Children

BlockSweep is not directed to children. It is rated for users aged 13 and over, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the App, please contact us and we will assist in removing any associated data.

5. Data retention and deletion

Because we do not receive or store your data ourselves, there is nothing on our side to delete. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored game data. For data held by Google in connection with advertising, please refer to the Google links in section 2, or contact us at the address below and we will help you direct the request.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above and publish the revised policy at this same address before the change takes effect.

7. Contact

3. 이용자의 선택권

기기에서 설정 → Google → 광고 경로로 광고 ID를 언제든지 재설정하거나 삭제할 수 있습니다. 광고 ID를 삭제하면 맞춤 광고가 중단되며, 광고 자체는 계속 표시되지만 관심사에 기반하지 않습니다.

유럽경제지역·영국·스위스 이용자에게는 맞춤 광고 이용 전 Google User Messaging Platform을 통해 동의를 요청하며, 동의는 앱 내에서 언제든지 변경하거나 철회할 수 있습니다.
