Privacy Policy for BlockSweep

Last updated: October 25, 2026

BlockSweep (“the App”) is developed and published by Deokgu House (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the App handles and why. We do not ask you to create an account with us, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or contacts. Signing in with Google Play Games (for the leaderboard) is optional.

1. Information collected

We ourselves do not operate any server and do not receive your personal data. However, the App displays advertising provided by Google AdMob, and the AdMob software development kit collects the following information from your device:

Data Purpose
Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) Serving ads, measuring ad performance, limiting how often the same ad is shown, and detecting fraud
Approximate location derived from IP address, device model, operating system version, language, screen size Selecting ads appropriate for the device and region
Ad interaction events (an ad was shown, an ad was tapped) Measuring ad performance and calculating payments
Diagnostic information (app launch time, hang rate, energy usage) Analytics and keeping the ad SDK stable

Your game progress — high score, current board, sound and language settings — is stored in the App’s local storage on your own device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

Leaderboard (Google Play Games Services, optional). Only if you choose to sign in with Google Play Games from the Ranking screen, the following is sent to and stored by Google so that the global leaderboard can work:

Data Purpose
Your Play Games player ID and gamer name (nickname) and avatar Identifying you on the leaderboard and showing your name next to your score
Your scores at the end of each game Ranking your scores on the leaderboard

The App reads the leaderboard (top players’ gamer names and scores, and your rank) from Google only to display it on screen; it does not store it or send it to us. Google does not share your email address or Google account details with the App. Whether other players can see your name and score depends on your Play Games profile visibility setting.

2. Third-party services

The App uses Google AdMob and Google Play Games Services. Google processes the data described above as an independent controller under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Play Games profile and privacy settings

3. Your choices

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time on your device: Settings → Google → Ads. Deleting the Advertising ID stops personalized advertising; you will still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before using your data for personalized advertising, through Google’s User Messaging Platform. You may change or withdraw that consent at any time from within the App (Options → Ad privacy options).

Signing in to Google Play Games is optional; the game can be played fully without it. You can make your Play Games profile private or change your gamer name at any time (in the App: Ranking → the button below the list, or in the Play Games settings). A private profile is not shown on the public leaderboard.

4. Children

BlockSweep is not directed to children. It is rated for users aged 13 and over, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the App, please contact us and we will assist in removing any associated data.

5. Data retention and deletion

Because we do not receive or store your data ourselves, there is nothing on our side to delete. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored game data. For data held by Google in connection with advertising or Google Play Games (including leaderboard scores), please refer to the Google links in section 2 — you can delete your Play Games profile and data in the Play Games settings — or contact us at the address below and we will help you direct the request.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above and publish the revised policy at this same address before the change takes effect.

본 앱은 Google AdMob과 Google Play 게임즈 서비스를 이용합니다. Google은 위 정보를 자체 개인정보처리방침에 따라 독립적으로 처리합니다.

  • Google Play 게임즈 프로필 및 개인정보 설정

3. 이용자의 선택권

기기에서 설정 → Google → 광고 경로로 광고 ID를 언제든지 재설정하거나 삭제할 수 있습니다. 광고 ID를 삭제하면 맞춤 광고가 중단되며, 광고 자체는 계속 표시되지만 관심사에 기반하지 않습니다.

유럽경제지역·영국·스위스 이용자에게는 맞춤 광고 이용 전 Google User Messaging Platform을 통해 동의를 요청하며, 동의는 앱 내에서 언제든지 변경하거나 철회할 수 있습니다(옵션 → 광고 개인정보 옵션).

Google Play 게임즈 로그인은 선택 사항이며, 로그인하지 않아도 게임의 모든 기능을 이용할 수 있습니다. Play 게임즈 프로필을 비공개로 하거나 게이머 이름을 언제든지 바꿀 수 있습니다(앱의 랭킹 화면 목록 아래 버튼, 또는 Play 게임즈 설정). 비공개 프로필은 공개 랭킹에 표시되지 않습니다.

본 앱은 아동을 대상으로 하지 않으며 만 13세 이상 이용가로 등급이 설정되어 있습니다. 개발자는 만 13세 미만 아동의 정보를 고의로 수집하지 않습니다. 아동이 본 앱을 이용한 정황이 있다면 아래 연락처로 알려 주시면 관련 데이터 삭제를 돕겠습니다.
